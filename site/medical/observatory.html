WHY THEY DISAGREE

A label crosses a boundary.
A score counts every distance.

Accuracy only asks which side of 0.5 a probability falls on. Brier score uses the distance between every probability and its recorded binary label. A calibration step can repair one label while making the overall probability error larger.

Read the uncertainty, including what it leaves out

The original run uses 2,000 seeded test-row bootstrap replicates for Brier and AUROC, with paired resampling for the Brier difference. These intervals condition on this fitted model and this split; they do not include retraining variability.

Wilson binomial intervals describe observed proportions. Corruption-mask ranges belong to the separate missingness study and are not population confidence intervals. Repeated masks reuse the same test cohort. No result here establishes subgroup performance, clinical utility, or a universal best model.
