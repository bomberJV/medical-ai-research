What counts as better?

Calibration changed one classification. For 112 of the 114 samples, the new probability moved farther from the recorded outcome.

How can both scores rise?

Accuracy counts the correct labels at the 50% cutoff. The Brier score averages the squared distance from each probability to its recorded outcome, 1 for malignant and 0 for benign. A smaller distance improves the score even when the label stays the same.

How much uncertainty is there?

The original run resampled the same 114 test records 2,000 times. It used paired samples for the Brier difference. These intervals describe the fitted models on this split and exclude variation from retraining.
