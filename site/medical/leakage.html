01 / ONE COLUMN

How long a patient was followed tells you whether they died.

The dataset has a column called time: days of follow-up. For a patient who died, it is the day they died. For a survivor, it is how long the study watched them. Drag the cutoff and predict “died” for everyone followed less than that many days.

What to take from this

Without follow-up time, honest models on this dataset land around 74% accuracy (AUROC about 0.80). Reported accuracies of 90% and above are largely explained by follow-up time as an input, oversampling before the split, and reporting a single split, not by better models. If you use this dataset: drop time, or make it the outcome of a survival model, and oversample only inside training folds.

METHODS & DOWNLOADS

Check the work

We took all 764 works that cite the dataset's two source papers (OpenAlex, 15 November 2026), read the 241 we could get in full, and coded each classifier for how it used follow-up time. The experiments retrain random forests with and without each shortcut. Study-level codes will be published with names once a person has checked them.
